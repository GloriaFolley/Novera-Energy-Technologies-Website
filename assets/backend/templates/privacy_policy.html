Privacy Policy

Novera Energy & Technologies

Effective Date: 24th January 2026

Novera Energy & Technologies respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact us, request consultations, or apply for our training programmes.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we may collect, why we collect it, how we may use it, how we may protect it, and the choices and rights available to individuals whose personal information we process.

This policy applies to personal information collected through the Novera website and through related communication channels connected to website enquiries.

Who We Are

Novera Energy & Technologies is a Ghanaian security, electrical, automation, communication, and technology installation company.

For privacy-related purposes, Novera may act as the organisation responsible for determining why and how personal information submitted through our website is processed.

Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Novera, we may collect information such as:

Consultation enquiries

When you submit a consultation request, we may collect:

  • full name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • company or organisation name, where provided;
  • service requested;
  • message or project description;
  • preferred or selected language; and
  • the date and time associated with the enquiry.

Training applications

Where Novera Academy applications are available, we may collect information such as:

  • full name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • selected training programme;
  • education information;
  • company or organisation information;
  • experience information;
  • motivation for applying; and
  • other information voluntarily supplied in the application.

Other communications

If you contact us by email, telephone, WhatsApp, social media, or another channel, we may process the information you provide, including your name, contact details, enquiry details, and communications history, to the extent reasonably necessary to respond to you and manage the relationship.

How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to consultation requests and enquiries;
  • understand a customer's service requirements;
  • prepare or discuss quotations and proposals;
  • arrange consultations, site visits, installations, maintenance, support, or other requested services;
  • manage customer communications;
  • process and review training applications;
  • maintain records of enquiries, applications, and business communications;
  • operate and secure our website and related systems;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, spam, or unauthorised activity;
  • comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, or record-keeping obligations; and
  • protect Novera's rights, property, systems, personnel, customers, and legitimate business interests.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected unless permitted or required by applicable law or otherwise appropriately authorised.

Legal and Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, Novera will process personal information on a lawful basis permitted by applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • your consent;
  • taking steps at your request in connection with a potential service or business relationship;
  • performing or preparing for a contract;
  • complying with a legal or regulatory obligation; or
  • pursuing a legitimate business purpose where permitted by law and where the rights and interests of the individual are appropriately considered.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable. Withdrawal of consent does not necessarily affect processing that was lawfully carried out before consent was withdrawn.

How We Share Personal Information

Novera may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Depending on the circumstances, information may be accessible to:

  • authorised Novera personnel who need the information to respond to an enquiry or perform their duties;
  • service providers supporting Novera's website, hosting, email, communications, information technology, or administrative operations;
  • contractors or professional service providers where necessary for legitimate business purposes;
  • manufacturers, suppliers, or technical partners where sharing is necessary to evaluate or deliver a requested service; and
  • government authorities, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Novera does not intend to sell customers' personal information as a commercial product.

Email, WhatsApp and Third-Party Communication Platforms

When you choose to contact Novera through WhatsApp, email, social media, telephone, or another third-party communication platform, your information may also be processed by that third-party provider under its own privacy policy and terms.

Novera cannot control the privacy practices, security measures, availability, or processing performed by those external providers.

Website Security

Novera takes reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, loss, or destruction.

However, no website, database, network, email transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for the purpose of your enquiry.

Data Retention

Novera will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining customer and business records, managing applications, resolving disputes, protecting our interests, and meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

Where specific retention periods are required by law, those requirements will apply.

Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including the right to:

  • request information about the processing of their personal information;
  • request access to personal information held about them;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion or removal of personal information where applicable;
  • object to certain forms of processing where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • raise a complaint concerning the handling of personal information.

Requests may be subject to reasonable identity verification and any legal exceptions that apply.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To make a privacy or data-protection request, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below.

Please describe your request clearly and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant enquiry or record.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how Novera has handled your personal information, please contact us first so that we can review and address the matter.

Where applicable, you may also have the right to raise a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority in Ghana.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Novera may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve parts of the website.

Where non-essential cookies or similar technologies are introduced in the future, Novera may update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Novera's website and services are intended for general business and consumer use and are not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the service or communication requested.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Novera without appropriate authorisation, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

International Processing

Some service providers used by Novera may process or store information outside Ghana.

Where personal information is transferred or processed across borders, Novera will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social-media platforms, manufacturers, or other services.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to review their own privacy policies before submitting personal information to them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Novera may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, systems, business practices, legal requirements, or data-protection practices.

When we make significant changes, we may update the effective date shown at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide an additional notice.
